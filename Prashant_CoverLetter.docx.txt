3. What’s the craziest thing you’ve ever done? The story your friends still tell.
During my third year of college, I was the Operations Head of India's largest student-run social organisation, Abhyuday, IIT Bombay. I was responsible for organising our annual festival, which amasses 11,000 attendees every year.


Early that morning, our labour vendor, responsible for setting up essential infrastructure, backed out. After an initial moment of panic, I immediately began calling friends, juniors, and batchmates to help with the setup. Incentives, Favors, Pleas and whatnot.
One showed up, and then another one. By the time the goods truck arrived, over 50 people had assembled, ready to help. Together, we completed the venue setup just before the event began. Every time we meet, this surely remains the most memorable experience for our team.
