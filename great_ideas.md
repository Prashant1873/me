# Great Ideas

Valuable concepts, design directions, and feature ideas for the portfolio.

## Design Direction
- **"Structured Intensity"** is the brand: industrial editorial minimalism + cinematic warmth
- Dark mode as signature; light mode as working/reading mode
- Orbital geometry motif = aerospace background + multiple interests orbiting coherent center
- Never compete with content - motifs support information, not replace it

## 3D Vision
- Hero orbital canvas: concentric circles with intersecting trajectories - feels like a command center
- Experience 3D horizontal spatial timeline: integrated full-width X-axis linear track with authentic company brand logo medallions, rim lighting, specular depth, energy conduit, and attached active milestone cards
- Minimalist color-coded calendar modal: clean year-by-year 2024-2026 month blocks styled with signature company brand palettes, eliminating heavy text clutter
- Terrain landscape: weathered stone aesthetic as scrollable background narrative
- Particle systems: subtle, ethereal cosmic dust motes (300 motes) and luminous energy pulses along conduit
- Per-section objects: architectural/geometric forms that reinforce each section's theme

## Storytelling Arc
- The website tells Prashant's story linearly: who he is → what he's done → what he's built → where he learned → how to reach him
- Hero narrative: "thinker-builder hybrid" - behavioral psychology + automation obsession
- Not a resume on screen - a cinematic experience of meeting someone

## Technical Ideas
- GSAP ScrollTrigger for all scroll-linked animations - industry standard, best performance
- Full-viewport elastic section scroll: single long scroll advances exactly one section end-to-end with Apple fluid spring deceleration, rubber-band boundary bounce, zero-lag trackpad accumulator (14px threshold), and interruptible mid-flight reversals & chaining
- Three.js scenes should share a single WebGL renderer for performance
- Progressive enhancement: detect GPU capability, simplify 3D for mobile
- Prefers-reduced-motion: disable all 3D and transitions, show clean static layout
- Zero-FOUC skeleton shell: instant frame-0 render with render-blocking CSS and smooth 3D handoff
- Authentic artifact evidence: subtle inline photo proof strips for real-world roles from `Portfolio_images`
- Education lineage: St. Francis' College (ICSE 98% Gold Medal) → City Montessori School / CMS (ISC 98.25% Gold Medal) → IIT Bombay (Aerospace B.Tech)

## Milestone 2 Visual Polish Directions
- **3D Orbital Z-Occlusion & Scaling:** Experience particle sizes substantially larger for unmistakable presence in both light and dark modes; particles swinging behind the medallion along the z-axis dynamically attenuate/vanish behind the logo disc.
- **Borderless Dark Calendar:** Replace pastel blocks and rigid cell outlines with borderless, rich, theme-coherent dark blocks.
- **Project Backdrop Legibility:** Soften card background blur/dimming so product screenshots yield an identifiable preview; vertically center 2x3 cards with tight, uniform vertical spacing.
- **Scannable Governance Cards:** Strip verbose body prose from Abhyuday and Placement Cell cards; elevate prominent bold key metrics.
- **Vector / Favicon Iconography:** Replace emoji markers (🎭, 🎙️, 🏑, 🥇) with razor-sharp SVG / favicon glyphs.
- **Global Anchor Y-Alignment:** Identical vertical Y position for all page titles with deliberate top breathing room.

## Milestone 3 Performance, Contrast, Pedigree & Mobile UX
- **Experience Photo Pipeline:** WebP-compressed responsive assets (17.8MB to 1.4MB, 92.1% payload reduction), instant stale-image reset, skeleton loaders, and proactive adjacent asset preloading.
- **Project Card Contrast & Scrims:** Deepened backdrop linear gradients, radial protective scrims, micro-capsule pill tags, and guaranteed WCAG AA readability.
- **Education Card Interior Overhaul:** 3 equal columns for Academic Excellence, clean metric chips without spreadsheet-box clutter, clean "2× GOLD MEDALIST" pill without flanking emoji/medal icons, and 26+ resume proficiencies filling Core Competencies.
- **Contact Editorial Single Sentence:** "Architecting category-defining product strategies from first principles and forging high-leverage systems that turn ambitious visions into market-defining products people revere."
- **Tactile Mobile Drawer Cards:** Tactile glassmorphic command-module cards with index counter numerals, micro-chevrons, and spring physics micro-interactions.
